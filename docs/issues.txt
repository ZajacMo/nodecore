- Tote handles should be recyclable.  Need to detect and make sure
  they're empty though!

- Injury and Radiation can stack to bring inventory slots down
  to zero.  Need to pre-calculate all amounts and reduce
  proportionally to ensure at least 2 slots stay free.

- Recipes that suck:
	- Tote recipe has not aged well.
	- Door chiseling is a bit too cumbersome.
		- Pummel with a pick?
	- Door hinge pounding is too cumbersome.
		- Don't require graphite.
		- Just righ-click, no pounding?
		- Must access an end surface?

- Doors should prefer centrifugal ejection unless
  blocked outside.
  	- This would also be more consistent with
 	  the only sane way to do in-path obstacle ejection,
	  because there's no way to force centrifugal
	  ejection in that case otherwise.

	- Dirt cycle, make peat, compost to humus.
	- Craft and light torches.
	- Write with charcoal.

		- Hold sneak

	- Change how scaling handholds are visualized; maybe a
	  slow particle effect?  Remove directionality.
